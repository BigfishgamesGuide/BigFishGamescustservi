How to Reach Big Fish Games Customer Service?
=============================================

If you need help with a Big Fish Games account, purchase, game, billing issue, Game Club membership, or technical problem, you can contact Big Fish Games Customer Service through the company's official Help Center.

Big Fish Games provides support articles for common problems and allows customers to submit a support request when they cannot find an answer. The company currently states that typical responses take around 24 to 72 hours, although its Help Center also notes that the current first-response time is roughly 2 to 3 business days. [1]_


Start With the Big Fish Games Help Center
-----------------------------------------

The easiest way to reach Big Fish Games Customer Service is through the official Big Fish Games Help Center.

The Help Center contains separate sections for account and sign-in problems, billing and purchasing, technical issues, Big Fish Game Club, mobile games, and other frequently asked questions. [2]_

Before contacting Customer Service, search the Help Center for your particular problem. You may find a step-by-step solution without needing to submit a support request.

For example, you can search for:

* ``Forgot password``
* ``Game will not install``
* ``Game crashes``
* ``Game will not download``
* ``Missing purchased game``
* ``Billing problem``
* ``Game Club problem``
* ``Login problem``

If the available help articles do not resolve your issue, you can proceed to the customer-support request form.


How to Reach Big Fish Games Customer Service
--------------------------------------------

Big Fish Games provides a ``Submit a request`` option through its Help Center. This is the main online method for contacting Customer Service.

Follow these steps:

#. Open the official Big Fish Games Help Center.
#. Search for your issue using a few relevant keywords.
#. Read the available troubleshooting articles.
#. If you still need assistance, select ``Submit a request``.
#. Provide the requested information.
#. Select the category that best matches your issue.
#. Describe the problem clearly and completely.
#. Attach screenshots or other files if they are relevant.
#. Review your information before submitting the request.
#. Submit the request.
#. Save the confirmation email for your records.

Big Fish Games states that after you submit the form, you will receive an automated email confirming that your question was received. A Customer Service response is then sent to the email address you provided. [1]_


Important Account Verification Requirement
------------------------------------------

Big Fish Games introduced an additional verification requirement for customers who submit support requests without signing in to their Help Center account.

Starting March 31, 2026, customers who submit a request anonymously through the Help Center must verify their email address before an active support ticket is created. Big Fish Games explains that the Help Center has a separate sign-in system from the regular Big Fish Games website. [3]_

If you submit a ticket while not signed in, check your email for the verification message and click the verification link. Your request may not reach Customer Service as an active ticket until the email verification process has been completed.

To avoid this additional step, you can create a Help Center account and sign in before submitting your request. [3]_


What Information Should You Provide?
------------------------------------

Providing complete information can help Customer Service understand your problem and investigate it more efficiently.

The information you should provide depends on the type of problem.

For most support requests, consider including:

* Your name, when requested.
* Your Big Fish Games account email address.
* The name of the affected game.
* A detailed description of the problem.
* Error messages or error codes.
* Your device or computer information.
* Your operating system.
* Troubleshooting steps you have already tried.
* Relevant order numbers.
* Screenshots showing the problem.
* Diagnostic information requested by Customer Service.

Do not include unnecessary personal or financial information.

A detailed description is particularly important. Instead of writing ``My game doesn't work``, explain what happens when you try to launch or play the game.


Contact Customer Service About Billing Problems
-----------------------------------------------

Billing and account questions are common reasons to contact Big Fish Games Customer Service.

If your problem involves a purchase, payment, refund, or billing charge, provide the relevant order number whenever possible.

Big Fish Games recommends providing information such as:

* A description of the billing problem.
* Relevant order number or numbers.
* Billing address.
* Alternate email addresses that may have been used to purchase games.
* The first four and last four digits of the payment card, when relevant. [1]_

Never send your complete credit-card number through a support request.

For example, if you need help identifying a payment, explain the date and amount of the transaction and provide the relevant order number. This gives Customer Service useful information without unnecessarily exposing sensitive payment information.


Contact Customer Service About Technical Problems
-------------------------------------------------

If a Big Fish Games title will not install, download, launch, or run correctly, you can contact Customer Service for technical assistance.

Big Fish Games provides a dedicated Technical Help section covering PC, Mac, Game Manager, installation, downloading, error codes, and other technical problems. [4]_

Before submitting a request, check the relevant troubleshooting article.

When contacting Customer Service about a technical problem, include:

* The title of the game.
* A description of the problem.
* The error code or message.
* Your operating system.
* The steps that cause the problem.
* Troubleshooting steps you have already attempted.
* A Dr. Felix Diagnostics Report for PC or Mac issues, if requested. [1]_

For example, instead of saying ``the game crashes``, explain when the crash occurs.

You could write::

   The game launches successfully but closes after the loading
   screen. I have restarted the computer and reinstalled the
   game, but the problem continues.

This type of description gives Customer Service a clearer understanding of the issue.


Contact Customer Service About Account Problems
------------------------------------------------

If you cannot sign in to your Big Fish Games account, check the Account/Sign-in Help section before submitting a request. Big Fish Games provides information about forgotten passwords, login errors, account recovery, and related issues. [5]_

If you still cannot access your account, submit a support request and explain the problem.

Useful information can include:

* The email address associated with your account.
* The exact login problem.
* Any error message displayed.
* Whether you can receive password-reset emails.
* Whether you recently changed your email address.
* Information about previously purchased games.

If you have an older account or believe that your previous purchases are missing, explain this clearly in the request. Providing additional account information can help Customer Service identify the correct account.


Contact Customer Service About Game Club
----------------------------------------

Big Fish Game Club customers can contact Customer Service for questions involving membership, payments, purchases, or other subscription-related issues.

When submitting a request, explain the specific problem rather than simply saying that you need help with Game Club.

For example, you may need assistance because:

* You have a question about your membership.
* You were charged unexpectedly.
* You want information about a Game Club purchase.
* Your payment method is not working.
* You have a question about cancellation.
* A purchased game is not appearing correctly.

Include relevant order information whenever possible.


Contact Customer Service for Mobile Games
------------------------------------------

Big Fish Games also supports mobile and free-to-play games. The information needed for mobile-game problems can differ from that required for PC or Mac games.

Big Fish Games recommends including your in-game identification information when requesting help with supported free-to-play games. Depending on the game, this may be called a User ID, AccountID, or Friend Code. [6]_

If you cannot find your in-game ID, include your email address and as much information about your game profile as possible.

Useful information can include:

* Game name.
* Player or profile name.
* Level reached.
* In-game ID.
* Friend Code, where applicable.
* Approximate date and time of the problem.
* Amount of in-game currency involved.
* Description of the problem.

This information can help Customer Service locate the correct game profile.


How Long Does Big Fish Games Customer Service Take?
----------------------------------------------------

Big Fish Games states that its average response time is typically around 24 to 72 hours. The company also notes that questions are answered in the order they are received, so response times can vary when support volume is high. [1]_

The current Help Center additionally states that the first response for support tickets is roughly 2 to 3 business days. [2]_

Because response times can vary, allow the stated processing period before submitting another request.

Sending repeated requests for the same issue is generally unnecessary and can make it harder to keep the support conversation organized.


What Happens After You Submit a Request?
-----------------------------------------

After submitting your request, Big Fish Games says you should receive an automated confirmation email.

This email confirms that Customer Service has received your question. A subsequent response from the support team will be sent to the email address associated with your request. [1]_

Keep the confirmation email until your issue has been resolved.

If Customer Service asks for additional information, reply to the existing conversation and provide the requested details.


What If Big Fish Games Does Not Reply?
---------------------------------------

If you are waiting for a response, first check your email folders.

Look in:

* Inbox.
* Spam.
* Junk Mail.
* Promotions.
* Other automatically filtered folders.

Big Fish Games specifically recommends checking Spam or Junk folders if you are waiting for a response. The company also recommends adding ``support@bfgentertainment.zendesk.com`` to your safe-senders list or address book to help prevent support emails from being filtered. [1]_

If you already submitted a support request, do not immediately create another request for the same problem.

Big Fish Games recommends replying to the confirmation email if you need to add information to an existing request. [1]_


How to Write a Good Customer Service Request
---------------------------------------------

A clear support request can make it easier for Customer Service to understand your situation.

Use a specific description of the problem.

For example::

   Subject: Game crashes after loading

   The game opens normally, but it closes immediately after
   the loading screen. The problem started yesterday. I am
   using Windows and have already restarted the computer and
   reinstalled the game. The issue continues after reinstalling.

This is more helpful than::

   Subject: Help

   My game doesn't work.

When writing your request, explain what happened, when the issue started, and what you have already tried.

If an error appears on your screen, copy the error message accurately.

If a screenshot would help explain the problem, attach one when the support form allows attachments.


How to Attach Files and Screenshots
-----------------------------------

Some support cases require additional files or screenshots.

For technical problems, screenshots can help Customer Service understand an error message, unexpected screen, installation problem, or other visual issue.

Big Fish Games provides separate Help Center resources covering file attachments and taking screenshots. [2]_

Only attach files that are relevant to your support request.

Before sending a screenshot, check that it does not accidentally expose unnecessary private information, such as passwords, complete payment information, or unrelated personal details.


Protect Your Account Information
--------------------------------

When contacting Big Fish Games Customer Service, protect your personal and financial information.

Never provide your Big Fish Games password.

Do not send your complete payment-card number.

For billing questions, follow the official support instructions regarding payment information. Big Fish Games specifically asks customers not to provide a full card number and instead identifies the first four and last four digits as useful information for certain billing cases. [1]_

Use the official Big Fish Games Help Center when looking for customer-service information. Be cautious about unofficial websites or individuals claiming to provide Big Fish Games support.

Frequently Asked Questions
---------------------------

How can I reach Big Fish Games Customer Service?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The primary method is to use the official Big Fish Games Help Center and submit a support request. Big Fish Games also lists a Phone Support resource in its Contact Us section. [2]_

How long does Big Fish Games take to respond?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Big Fish Games says its average response time is typically 24 to 72 hours. The current Help Center also indicates that first responses generally take around 2 to 3 business days. [1]_ [2]_

What should I include in my support request?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Include a detailed description of the problem, game title, error messages, troubleshooting steps, and relevant account or order information. Technical cases may require additional diagnostic information.

Can I send my complete credit-card number?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

No. Do not send the complete card number. Big Fish Games specifically advises customers not to share the full number. [1]_

What should I do if I do not receive a response?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Check your Spam and Junk folders. If you already received a confirmation email, reply to that existing email instead of submitting another request. [1]_

Do I need to verify my email address?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

If you submit a support request anonymously, Big Fish Games requires email verification before an active ticket is created. This requirement began March 31, 2026. [3]_

Can I contact support about a mobile game?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Yes. For mobile and free-to-play games, provide the game name and relevant player information. If available, include the game's in-game ID, such as a User ID, AccountID, or Friend Code. [6]_


Conclusion

Reaching Big Fish Games Customer Service is easiest through the official Help Center. Start by searching the available support articles because many common account, billing, technical, and game-related problems already have solutions.

Big Fish Games currently reports typical response times of approximately 24 to 72 hours, with the Help Center describing current first-response times as roughly 2 to 3 business days. [1]_ [2]_

By using the official Help Center, providing accurate information, and keeping your support conversation in one ticket, you can make the process of reaching Big Fish Games Customer Service simpler and more efficient.
